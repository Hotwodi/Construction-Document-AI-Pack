Construction Document AI Pack

Bid, Change Order & Invoice Extraction

$399.99 USD

The Construction Document AI Pack brings AI-powered document extraction to your Odoo construction workflow. Upload bids, change orders, invoices, certificates of insurance, delivery tickets, and subcontract documents — the module extracts key fields automatically, assigns per-field confidence scores, and routes everything through a human-in-the-loop review process.

Key Features

Bid Analysis

AI completeness scoring, missing-item detection, risk flags, and recommended actions for every uploaded bid.

Change Order Extraction

Pull change order numbers, amounts, schedule impact days, and AI approval recommendations with a single click.

Invoice Extraction

Extract vendor, invoice number, line items, subtotal, tax, and total. AI matches invoices to purchase orders with a confidence score.

Certificate of Insurance Tracker

Track vendor COIs with automatic expiry detection, days-until-expiry computation, and alert sending for expiring or expired policies.

Per-Field Confidence & Review

Every extracted field carries an AI confidence score. Reviewers can verify or correct individual fields before marking a job reviewed.

Delivery Tickets & Subcontracts

Built-in document types for delivery tickets and subcontracts so every construction document flows through the same pipeline.

Document Types Supported

  • Bid — completeness scoring, risk flags, recommended action
  • Change Order — amount, schedule impact, approval recommendation
  • Invoice — line items, totals, PO matching
  • Certificate of Insurance — expiry tracking and alerts
  • Delivery Ticket — field extraction and verification
  • Subcontract — field extraction and verification

Installation

  1. Place the construction_document_ai_pack folder in your Odoo addons directory.
  2. Restart the Odoo server.
  3. Activate Developer Mode and update the Apps list.
  4. Search for “Construction Document AI” and click Install.
